# 📈 The Pursuit of Progress Manifesto

> Copied from the Neocreo repo (`docs/gtm/manifestos/pursuit-of-progress-manifesto.md`, v0.1 seeded draft) on 2026-11-15. **Snapshot.** Until the Neocreo repo adopts this core, NC is the source: refresh this copy from NC instead of editing it here. Strategy context: [house of brands](README.md).

### 1 · Why it exists — the mission
To help operators and providers make **real progress over perfect** — the consulting/program studio (HubSpot Harmony, GTM Growth, B2B HumOps) that makes the relationship map of a business visible, usable, and human. Because **most GTM problems are relationship problems**, and a database is just a map of what exists, how it connects, and what needs attention.

### 2 · What it believes
- **Progress > perfection.** *"You're not behind — you're just doing it the hard way."*
- Most GTM problems are **relationship** problems, not tactics problems.
- **GTM is 6 connected objects** (Product, Content, Partner, Community, Event, Rep), reinforcing each other — "an event is not just an event."
- Progress is incremental and **custom to each person**, but rests on universal biological pillars.
- Work with rhythm, not grind (**HumOps** — help the organism operate better).

### 3 · Who it's truly for + the transformation
**Growth-obsessed operators, educators, and marketers** — and specifically the **systems/structure coach running a paying program on duct-tape** (the beachhead ICP). **Before:** stuck, perfection-paralyzed, GTM scattered across disconnected tools and tactics. **After:** a connected system, momentum, unstuck — running real campaigns across the 6 motions.

### 4 · The enemy
Perfection-paralysis. Hustle / grind culture. GTM-as-vanity-tactics. Duct-taped, disconnected operations. *(Guardrail: PoP is not here to out-build HubSpot's core — it makes existing objects work together, and dogfoods on Neocreo.)*

### 5 · Origin
Paul's RevOps decade and time at RevPartners; HumOps proven on himself first; and the **pursuit-of-progress paradigm** as the connective spine over everything — the thing that harmonizes every facet of life toward what lights you up.

### 6 · Paradigm + ladder-up
**Progress, via consulting** (RevOps · HumOps · GTM-as-6-objects · "Course → Program"). The sibling to *Neocreo* (Progress via software) and the **money engine** of the house (two tiers — see §7 of the brand guide). Ladders to *"progress, powered by dance"* → Paul.

### 7 · What it is NOT
Not a generic marketing agency. Not perfection or big-bang transformation. Not one-size-fits-all. **HH (top tier) is not a social demo funnel** — it's rep-closed, high-trust. Not a rival GTM-org competing with HubSpot's core.

### 8 · The feeling
*"I'm actually making progress."* Clarity. Unstuck. Human.
