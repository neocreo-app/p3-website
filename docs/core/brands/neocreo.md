## In one line

**The operating system for progress.** Life is a connected database; Neocreo makes it real, so
people and the providers who serve them can work *with* technology instead of against it.

## Why it exists

To make the "connected database of your life and work" real. *Your brand stays yours; Neocreo
powers the operating system underneath.*

## What it believes

- **Life is a connected database.** Everything relates to everything.
- **Opinionated beats configurable.** A sharp default is the product; configuration is a quiet
  escape hatch, never the headline.
- **Derive, don't demand.** The software figures things out from what you already have.
- **Work with your rhythms and biology,** not against them.
- **Tech is here to collaborate and solve problems,** not create more of them.

## Who it is for

- **Early builders who got the invite:** insiders in a new creative economy.
- **Providers and creators** running communities and programs (coaches, educators, consultants).
- **Before:** scattered, duct-taped, fighting ten apps. **After:** one connected, calm,
  human-readable source of truth.
- The qualifier is **trajectory, not income:** "what are you moving toward?" Never screen on
  revenue or imply a price floor.

## The enemy

Fragmented tool-sprawl. Generic, do-everything-poorly, configure-it-yourself SaaS. Tech that
extracts attention. Guardrail: Neocreo is the OS **underneath**, never a rival CRM or a HubSpot
clone.

## The feeling

**Insider. Calm, not dense. "My whole life, finally connected." Empowered.**

## Messaging

**Core pattern:** Neocreo turns scattered lived knowledge into guided operating paths.

1. Name what feels scattered.
2. Name the human or business trying to make progress.
3. Name the guided structure Neocreo creates.
4. Name what becomes clearer, more repeatable, or more trustworthy.

- **Copy formula:** "From scattered [thing] to a guided [path / system / rhythm / program] you
  can actually follow."
- **Trust formula:** "Turn [the way you already work / help / live] into a repeatable path,
  without losing the humanity of it."
- Do not paste one slogan everywhere; each area expresses the same change in its own words.

**Lines that already work:**

- "Your brand stays yours. Neocreo powers the operating system underneath." (providers)
- "The generic tools make you model everything yourself. Neocreo ships the model." (vs.
  Notion / Airtable / ClickUp)
- **"What era are you in?"** Lands in person; people pause and get excited. Eras → Sprints is
  unique to Neocreo.
- "Comprehensive → simple": hold every building block of progress inside one familiar UI and one
  legible story.

**Brand principles:** Human before system · Structure without sterility · Connection over lists ·
Body to business (personal Rhythms scale to business GTM) · Proof through Paul's own use.

## Voice

- Warm but structured. Not cold SaaS, not fluffy wellness.
- Confident, not hypey: say what it does.
- Insider and unapologetic about being invite-based; never apologize for the exclusivity.
- Use: see, connect, visualize, guide, structure, build, track.

## What it is NOT

- Not a HubSpot replacement or rival CRM.
- **Not open self-serve signup.** Access is by application or invite, tied to trust.
- Not the umbrella every program must join (your brand stays yours).
- Not a throwaway account; a real operating space for real progress data.

## The product today (for public pages)

The website's June V1 led with five objects (Rhythms, Goals, Projects, Programs, Assessments).
The product has grown well past that. As of November 2026 it includes Goals and Targets, Eras and
Sprints, Projects and Tasks, Rhythms, Programs, Courses, Assessments, Communities, Events, a
Library (Resources and Dashboards), and GTM Strategy. Every object shares one kit of views
(Table, List, Kanban, Gallery, Calendar, Dashboard) and typed fields.

`⚠️ open, Paul + Jas:` which objects the public site leads with now. Do not invent features; check

## Access and pricing (do not publish without Paul)

- The ladder is **Free → Founder** (Founder: $25/mo for 3 seats, aimed at founding teams). "Solo"
  and "Team" are retired; never quote them.
- Free is a ladder, not a crippled trial: getting organized is what makes the next step make
  sense.
- Pricing is deferred on the website until Paul asks for it.

## The path and the asks

- **Primary hard CTA: apply for an account / get an invite.**
- Jas's June call for the Neocreo destination: a landing page with **two doors**: (1) book a demo
  call, (2) create an account (apply). `⚠️ open:` the live site has only the apply door.

## Visual identity

Source of truth: **Jas's Neocreo guidelines in Canva** (see [README](README.md)); some elements also
in HubSpot's brand kit and the app's Brand records.

- **Accent: cyan.** App tokens: `--brand` `#0277b5` (light) / `#67d7ff` (dark). The marketing site
  uses `#22d3ee` on a dark `#070c18` page. `⚠️ open:` reconcile the website and app cyans in Canva.
- The app now has **light and dark themes**. The website is dark-only by choice; the June brief's
  "the app has no light mode" is out of date.
- Font: Inter. Headlines extra-bold with tight leading; body regular with relaxed leading. No
  decorative fonts: the structure is the personality.
- Icons: **Phosphor** only, matching the app.
- Wordmark: "Neo" in cyan, "creo" in white. Logo files in the app repo: `public/logo-icon.png`,
  `public/logo-full.png`, `docs/references/logo/Neocreo-main logo.png`. (The June brief's
  `docs/Logo/` path no longer exists.)
