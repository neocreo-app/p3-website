> Distilled from the Neocreo repo's `docs/gtm/brand-gtm-guide.md` (strategy), the brand
> manifestos, the Q3 content syncs with Jas (June 2026), and the Q4 2026 GTM map. The full
> strategy (money mechanics, offers, pricing) stays in the Neocreo repo; this file is what a
> website or a piece of content needs.

## The one story

> **It's all the pursuit of progress, harmonizing every facet of your life toward what lights you
> up. And the engine underneath isn't grind. It's rhythm and regulation, and dance is how you
> ignite it. You dance so you can progress.**

- **Progress** means harmony and aliveness, **not** optimization.
- **Dance powers progress.** It is the regulation and rhythm underneath, not a separate hobby.
- Public word: **"Dance"**. Signature brand verb: **"Prance"**.

## Four brands, each standing on its own

A page or a post wears **one** brand. That brand decides the audience, the voice, and the ask.

| Brand | Paradigm | For | Feeling | Primary ask | Owner | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🦌 **Primal Prancing** | Dance, instructed and practiced | People craving permission to move freely | Judgment-free playground; dance is medicine, not performance | Come to class / free intro to dance | Paul (Jas = community face) | [primal-prancing.md](primal-prancing.md) |
| 🐈 **BASS CATS** | Dance, experienced and felt | The EDM and creative crew | Your people. Underground belonging. Playful. Alive. | Join the BASS CATS Creative Club | **Jas** | [bass-cats.md](bass-cats.md) |
| 🌱 **Neocreo** | Progress, via software | Early builders who got the invite; providers running programs | Insider. Calm, not dense. "My whole life, finally connected." | Apply for an account / get an invite | Paul (Jas content-izes) | [neocreo.md](neocreo.md) |
| 📈 **Pursuit of Progress** | Progress, via consulting | Operators and providers making progress over perfect | "You're not behind, you're doing it the hard way." | Book a call | Paul (Jas runs GTM Growth delivery) | [pursuit-of-progress.md](pursuit-of-progress.md) |

**Two kinds of hub.** Neocreo is the *product* hub, the tool several brands' people land inside.
Paul is the *brand* hub, the human thread that makes them one world. Do not write "everything
funnels to Neocreo"; let each brand stand alone.

**Paul, the connective brand.** The thread through all four has its own file,
[`paul.md`](paul.md), and its own site (p3). Which site wears which brand:
[`../web/README.md`](../web/README.md#the-sites-branded-house-over-a-house-of-brands).

**Family resemblance, not sameness.** Primal Prancing and BASS CATS share DNA (one idea can wear
both hats with two edits). A brand's own site does not explain the other three unless asked.

## Visual identity: where the truth lives

- **Source of truth: Jas's brand guidelines in Canva** (templates, fonts, colors per brand).
- Some elements also live in **HubSpot's brand kit** and in **Neocreo Brands** (the app's Brand
  records). If those disagree with Canva, **Canva wins**; fix the other one.
- In code, every color and font is a **named token defined once** (CSS custom properties), never
  a hex value typed into a component. A brand swap should be a token change.

## The CTA heat ladder (from the Q3 content sync)

- **Soft** (every piece): save, share, comment, read more.
- **Medium** (weekly-ish): follow, sign up for updates, tag a friend.
- **Hard** (strategic): join, apply, book a call. The page or post must earn it.
