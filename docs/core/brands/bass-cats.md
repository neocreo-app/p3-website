## In one line

An underground dance collective for EDM and creative people: **a crew, not a course.**

## Why it exists

To give creative and EDM people a crew: a place to belong to the scene **and** keep loving the
music for the rest of their lives. The felt, experienced side of dance.

## What it believes

- The scene is about **belonging**, not performance or clout.
- You can love the music at 80. The point is a **sustainable** container for the energy. The 2am
  burnout is what we grew out of, not what we sell.
- **Crew > course.** People want their people in the scene, not another thing to consume.

## Who it is for

The EDM and creative crew. Jas pictures: **artists, music artists and performers, small business
owners, the EDM dance community**, starting with Paul and Jas's own network (easy to reach).

- **Before:** burning out on scene life, or on the outside of it. Lonely inside the crowd.
- **After:** a crew that is theirs. Energy in a container their body can hold.

## What we push against

- The unsustainable 2am-burn version of scene life.
- Loneliness inside the crowd.
- Gatekept, cliquey scenes.

## The feeling

**Your people. Underground belonging. Playful. Alive.**

Everything should leave someone thinking *"that's me, those are my people."*

## Voice

- Sounds like talking to your best friends. Warm, playful, a little underground. No corporate
  stiffness.
- Belonging over hype. Invite people in; do not sell at them.
- Short and felt. A line someone would say out loud at a show beats a paragraph.
- The scene's language, used honestly. If Jas would not say it, cut it.
- *"People just want to talk to another human being."* (Jas) Build toward real connection, not a
  content feed.

## Origin

Born from the scene Paul and Jas love. Paul was a Bass Cat: up till 2am, loving the music against
his own biology. BASS CATS keeps the love and drops the burnout, and it is the felt side of dance
that feeds Primal Prancing's taught practice. Jas is cofounder and the community face.
`⚠️ open:` Paul's own "why BASS CATS was formed and how it helped Primal Prancing" story, in his
voice.

## What it is NOT

- **Not a course** or a class. That is Primal Prancing: dance taught. BASS CATS is dance felt.
- **Not the concert or festival business.** Shelved and gated. No ticketing.
- **Not a party-hard brand.** Celebrate the music and the people, not the burnout.
- **Not Paul's time sink.** Jas-owned; runs without Paul.

1. **The magazine:** read digitally on basscats.org (the magazine viewer).
