# 🦌 The Primal Prancing Manifesto

> Copied from the Neocreo repo (`docs/gtm/manifestos/primal-prancing-manifesto.md`, v0.1 seeded draft) on 2026-11-15. **Snapshot.** Until the Neocreo repo adopts this core, NC is the source: refresh this copy from NC instead of editing it here. Strategy context: [house of brands](README.md).

### 1 · Why it exists — the mission
To give people **permission to move freely** — to rediscover dance as *medicine, not performance*. Primal Prancing exists so that moving your body with other people becomes a sustainable source of joy and regulation again, not a workout to survive or a performance to be judged.

### 2 · What it believes
- Dance is **medicine** — it regulates you, it's not a skill test.
- You don't have to be "good" to move. Joy beats aesthetics, every time.
- Movement is the **regulation layer** underneath a good life (the middle of the Personal Confidence pyramid) — you dance so you can progress.
- Working *with* your energy beats grinding against it.

### 3 · Who it's truly for + the transformation
**Prancers** — people craving permission to move freely (Boulder, local first). Someone who feels self-conscious in their body, who thinks dance is for "dancers," who's tired of punishment-fitness. **Before:** disconnected, judged, moving only to burn calories. **After:** moving for joy, regulated, part of a room that has their back.

### 4 · The enemy
Performance culture. Mirror-and-judgment studios. Dance gatekept to the "already good." Fitness-as-punishment.

### 5 · Origin
Paul at 34, finding dance fitness — a container to bring his love of dance to the world *without burning himself down* (the 2am-Bass-Cat → 5am-prancer inversion; HumOps run on himself first). Personal salvation, then shared.

### 6 · Paradigm + ladder-up
**Dance, instructed & practiced.** The taught/embodied expression of the Dance paradigm (BASS CATS is its felt/experienced sibling). Dance is the engine under Progress → ladders to *"progress, powered by dance"* → to Paul, the connective brand.

### 7 · What it is NOT
Not a performance troupe. Not weight-loss/aesthetics fitness. Not skill-gated or exclusive. **Not a revenue line** — the community is the funnel; revenue comes later via the *Prancing Confidence* product on Neocreo.

### 8 · The feeling
Permission. Joy. Safety. *"I can just move, and it's enough."*
