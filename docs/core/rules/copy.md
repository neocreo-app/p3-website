# Copy rules (every brand)

For any words a visitor or user reads: website copy, product copy, emails, posts. Each brand's
**voice** is in its own file in [`../brands/`](../brands/); these rules sit underneath all of
them. Run `/copy-pass` on new or changed copy before shipping.

## House rules

1. **No em dashes.** Use a period, comma, or colon.
2. **Neocreo only: bold section titles,** not tiny uppercase "eyebrow" labels above headings.
   Other brands follow their own Canva guidelines.
3. **Exact brand names.** Neocreo (one capital N), BASS CATS, Primal Prancing, Pursuit of
   Progress. See [`../brands/README.md`](../brands/README.md#names-exact-spelling).
4. **Neocreo only: capitalize object names in prose** ("a Goal", "your Rhythms") but leave ordinary
   English alone ("the rhythm of the week" is not the Rhythms object).
5. **No hype words:** transform, revolutionize, supercharge, game-changing, unlock, elevate,
   seamless, cutting-edge.
6. **Say what it does.** Confident, not hypey. If a claim needs "very" or "truly", it needs a
   fact instead.
7. **One idea per section,** and **one clear next step per page.**
8. **Do not invent features, numbers, or quotes.** If unsure, ask, or mark `⚠️ open:` in a draft.

## Before copy ships (from the house content checklist)

- **Which brand is it wearing?** One brand per page or piece.
- **Who is it for?** Does it make that person think "that's me"?
- **What is the next step?** And is the CTA heat right (soft / medium / hard), with the hard ask
  earned?
- **Voice check:** does it sound like us talking to our best friends? No corporate stiffness.

## Keep our words

Paul and Jas's phrases carry meaning: "crew, not a course", "your brand stays yours", "what era
are you in?", "progress, powered by dance". Do not flatten them into generic marketing language.
