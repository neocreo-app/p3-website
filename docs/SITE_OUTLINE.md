## 5. Page by page

### Home
1. **Hero:** the hook, *"The operator whose engine is the dance floor,"* and one line on the
   pursuit of progress, powered by dance. No hard button; links to the story and the doors.
2. **The one story:** short. Progress means harmony and aliveness, not optimization. Dance is
   the regulation and rhythm underneath, not a separate hobby.
3. **Doors:** two paradigms, four cards, each linking out:
   - 🕺 Dance: Primal Prancing (primalprancing.com), BASS CATS (basscats.org)
   - 📈 Progress: Neocreo (neocreo.io), Pursuit of Progress (pursuitofprogress.org)
   Each card says who it's for in one line, in that brand's words.
4. **Who it's for:** people split between their structured, ambitious side and their creative,
   embodied side.
5. **Follow band:** Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube.

### Story
The founding story: the RevOps decade, the 2am Bass Cat becoming the 5am prancer, the builder
choosing to be seen. Photos from both worlds. Ends on the doors and the follow band.
